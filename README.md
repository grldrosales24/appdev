# 🎬 My Favorite Series and Movie

## 📺 Favorite Series: **Stranger Things**

**Stranger Things** is a science fiction horror series that takes place in the 1980s.  
It follows a group of kids who encounter mysterious government experiments and a creature from the *Upside Down.*

### ⭐ Why I Like It:
- Great storyline and suspense  
- Strong friendships between characters  
- 80s retro music and atmosphere

2. Mike Wheeler  
3. Jim Hopper

## 🎥 Favorite Movie: **Interstellar**

*Interstellar* is a science fiction film about astronauts traveling through a wormhole to save humanity.
